What we're proposing

Search that understands part numbers. An exact base part such as A1335 is pinned at the top with its key specs and the three things people come for: the datasheet, samples and availability. Orderable part numbers (A1335LLETR-T) resolve to the right row on the right part page. A competitor's part number returns the nearest Allegro equivalent.

Why

On the live site, searching A1335 puts an application note above the product, and the same note appears twice. Searching A1335LLETR-T, which is what a buyer or a distributor quote will contain, returns 1,672 loosely related records.

Engineers who already know the part are the easiest visitors to serve and the ones most likely to be ready for a sample or a quote.

BehaviourPart numbers are matched on prefix and normalised (case, spaces, hyphens, packaging suffixes), so "a1335 lletr" still finds A1335LLETR-T. Part numbers are the same in every language, so this works identically on the Chinese and Japanese sites.
