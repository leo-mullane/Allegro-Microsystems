What we're proposing

A guided finder for engineers who know the problem but not the component. It asks questions in the engineer's terms (what are you sensing, where does the magnet sit, what does your controller expect) rather than Allegro's catalogue terms, shows how many parts are left after every answer, and never offers an answer that would leave none. The answers are saved as a named design profile that follows the engineer round the site, marking parts that fit.

There is also a quicker way in from information the engineer already has: a competitor part number, or an Allegro part they use today.

Why

This is the Halfords idea. Nobody buying wiper blades is expected to know the catalogue; they enter their registration and the site filters everything to what fits the car, and keeps doing so on every page. An engineer's equivalent of the registration is a short set of constraints (market, safety level, supply, output) plus, very often, the part they are trying to replace.

Allegro has an AskAllegro assistant on the site already. A structured finder and an assistant aren't rivals: the finder's questions are the data an assistant needs to give a reliable answer.
