Allegro MicroSystems: product discovery

A greyscale prototype of how engineers could find, evaluate and buy Allegro parts, built around four ways people arrive: with a part number, a product type, a system they're designing, or a list of requirements. It's deliberately unstyled so the conversation stays on structure and behaviour.

Everything that should work, works: search, filters, comparison, the block diagram, the Product Finder and the samples and quote basket. Part data comes from Allegro's live angle sensor table; stock, prices beyond the one quoted on the live site, and cross-references are sample data.

The four journeys

Journey 1

“I know the exact part”

Part-number aware search, a part page built from the parts database, and availability, samples and quote by orderable part number.

Try: type A1335LLETR into the search box and pick the orderable part, then add a sample and open the basket. Change the region in the header and watch prices and distributors change.

Open journey 1
Journey 2

“I know what type of product I need”

The whole product range on one page with counts, then a filterable table and side-by-side comparison.

Try: Products → Magnetic Rotary Angle Sensors, filter to Steer-by-Wire and ASIL D, tick three parts and compare with "show differences only".

Open journey 2
Journey 3

“I know what I'm designing”

Applications as a separate taxonomy, a problem-first system page and a block diagram that hands over to the product table.

Try: on Steer-by-Wire, click "Handwheel angle sensor" in the diagram, then "See all" to land on a pre-filtered table.

Open journey 3
Journey 4

“I know the requirements, but not the component”

A guided finder with live counts, competitor cross-reference, and a design profile that follows you round the site.

Try: enter MLX90316 under "Already have a part number?", then answer the questions, save the design and see which parts fit.

Open journey 4

How to review

  • Use the dark bar at the top to move between journeys. It's a review tool, not part of the proposed site. Previous and next links at the foot of each page follow the same order.
  • Switch Notes on to see what we're proposing on each page and why, plus numbered notes on behaviour (yellow) and open questions for Allegro (red).
  • The header, menus and pages below the dark bar are the proposed site. Logos and images are labelled grey boxes on purpose.
  • Your choices (compare list, basket, design profile, region) are remembered while the browser tab is open.
