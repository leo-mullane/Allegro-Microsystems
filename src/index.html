Product discovery for Allegro MicroSystems

Prototype

Helping engineers find, judge and buy the right Allegro part.

A greyscale prototype of how product discovery could work on allegromicro.com, built around the four ways engineers arrive: with a part number, a product type, a system they're designing, or a list of requirements. The prototype pages are deliberately unstyled so the conversation stays on structure and behaviour.

THE FOUR JOURNEYSStart from what the engineer already knows

Everything that should work, works: search, filters, comparison, the system diagram, the Product Finder and the samples and quote basket. Part data comes from Allegro's live angle sensor table; stock, prices beyond the one quoted on the live site, and cross-references are sample data.

1

“I know the exact part”

Part-number aware search, a part page built from the parts database, and availability, samples and quote by orderable part number.

Try: type A1335LLETR into the search box and pick the orderable part, then add a sample and open the basket. Change the region in the header and watch prices and distributors change.

HOW TO REVIEWFinding your way round

  1. Use the dark bar at the top to move between journeys. It's a review tool, not part of the proposed site. Previous and next links at the foot of each page follow the same order.
  2. Switch Notes on to see what we're proposing on each page and why, plus numbered notes on behaviour (yellow) and open questions for Allegro (red).
  3. The header, menus and pages below the dark bar are the proposed site. Logos and images are labelled grey boxes on purpose.
  4. Your choices (compare list, basket, design profile, region) are remembered while the browser tab is open.
