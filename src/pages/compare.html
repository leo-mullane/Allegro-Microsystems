What we're proposing

Side-by-side comparison of up to four parts, with the rows that differ highlighted and an option to hide the rows that don't. Samples can be ordered from the comparison, because this is often the point where an engineer decides to try two candidates.

Why

There is no comparison on the live site, so today the comparison happens in a spreadsheet built from the CSV export or from datasheets opened in separate tabs. The data already exists to do it on the page.
