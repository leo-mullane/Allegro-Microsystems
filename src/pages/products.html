What we're proposing

A product taxonomy of no more than four levels (Family, Group, Product Type, Part), shown all at once on one page with live part counts, so an engineer can see the whole range and jump straight to the level they recognise. Every product type has a "view all parts" shortcut that skips the remaining levels and lands on a filterable table.

Why

On the live site, reaching the angle sensor table takes four clicks through pages that are mostly introductory copy (Products, Sensors, Position Sensors, Motor Position Sensors (2D)), and only at the last step does the table appear. The labels mix technology (TMR, Hall), dimensions (1D, 2D, 3D) and uses (Motor Position), which makes it hard to guess which branch a part is in.

Showing counts and a shortcut at every level is borrowed from Flowtech's catalogue, where it lets trade buyers move as fast as they already know the range.
