What we're proposing

One page per base part, at one short address (/products/a1335), built from the parts database. It answers four questions in order: is this the right part (key specs, status, fit with your design), can I trust it (datasheet as a web page as well as a PDF, safety documents), can I get it (every orderable version with stock, price at MOQ, samples, quote and distributors for your region) and what else should I look at (similar parts, the systems it is used in).

Why

The live A1335 page already has much of this, including price and stock behind each Buy button, which is a better start than most. But its address depends on the menu: the category table links to a legacy Angular Position Sensor ICs path that redirects to one under Motor Position Sensors, price and stock are hidden in a pop-up, and the Target Application links are not reflected back on the steer-by-wire page.

Putting the part at a flat address that never changes when the menu is reorganised is the single biggest thing for SEO and AI search, because every link and citation then points at one page.

Overview, documents and design tools below are the A1335's, shown as sample content.

The A1335 family of system-on-chip sensors delivers high-resolution, 360° contactless angle measurement. Integrated linearisation corrects magnetic non-linearities in both on-axis and off-axis designs. The family includes the AEC-Q100 qualified A1335 for automotive use and the A1335N for industrial use.

Top features

  • 360° contactless sensing with 12-bit resolution and 32 µs refresh rate
  • Circular Vertical Hall (CVH) technology for on- and off-axis applications
  • Selectable I²C, SPI and SENT (A1335 only) digital interfaces
  • On-chip harmonic and segmented linearisation
