What we're proposing

The homepage stops being mainly a news carousel and becomes a set of four doors, one for each way an engineer arrives: with a part number, with a product type, with a system they are designing, or with a list of requirements and no idea which part meets them.

All four doors lead to the same part pages and the same filtered tables, so there is one version of each part and one set of data behind it.

Why

Today's homepage leads with trade shows and press releases. That suits a visitor who wants news, but most visitors with a design to finish want a part, and the only route to one from the homepage is through the menu or a search box hidden behind an icon.

Searching the orderable part number A1335LLETR-T on the live site returns 1,672 records and the first one is a current sensor. The search box needs to be the most capable thing on the page, not the smallest.

Find the right part for your design

Search every Allegro sensor, power and motor driver ICs by part number, competitor part, product type or the system you're building.

BehaviourTypeahead groups results as Parts, Orderable part numbers, Cross-reference, Product types, Applications and Documents. Typing an exact part or orderable part number and pressing Enter goes straight to the part page, skipping the results list.
