What we're proposing

One basket with three kinds of line: free samples (what Allegro fulfils today), quote requests for production volumes, and, later, direct purchase. Every line is an orderable part number, which is the level at which stock, price, MOQ and packaging live. Lines can move between samples and quote, and a quote carries the design name from the finder.

Why

The live site already has a sample cart and a Buy panel with stock, price at MOQ and a distributor list, then sends the visitor to Contact Sales for anything else. That's a reasonable foundation. Putting samples, quotes and purchase in one structure now means adding checkout later is a new button and a payment step, not a rebuild of the product pages.

Flowtech's quote-to-order flow (request a quote, retrieve it by reference, convert it) is the model for B2B volumes.
